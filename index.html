What it won’t tell you, yet

We would rather you knew the edges of the data before you paid for it.

  • Works size. Flow and population figures are blank for almost every works in this edition. If we publish an edition that adds them, buyers of this one get it free.
  • Exact location. Positions are accurate to about a kilometre, which is enough to plan a visit and not enough to find the gate.
  • Who delivers each scheme. Not public yet. Southern Water’s AMP8 non‑infrastructure delivery partners are CMDP+ (Costain and MWH Treatment), Kier Integrated Services and Galliford Try, so the conversation starts with them.
  • A ranking that knows your business. Ours is a deliberately simple starting point. Every weighting is in the dictionary, so you can change it in minutes.
