What the pack can’t tell you yet

  • Works size. Flow and population figures are missing for almost every works in this version. We plan to add them in the next one.
  • Exact location. Positions are accurate to about a kilometre.
  • Who delivers each scheme. That isn’t public yet. Southern Water’s AMP8 non‑infrastructure delivery partners are CMDP+ (Costain and MWH Treatment), Kier Integrated Services and Galliford Try.
  • The perfect ranking. The rankings are a deliberately simple starting point. Every weighting is written down in the data dictionary, so you can re‑sort the list your own way.
