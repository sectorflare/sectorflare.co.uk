What this edition does not include

Stated here so you can judge the pack before you order it.

  • Works size. Flow and population figures are blank for almost every works in this edition; the public register that holds them was unavailable when we built it. Southern Water’s plan says 32 of the 95 serve fewer than 1,000 people, but not which. The second ranking therefore orders works by proposed limit, loosest first, for initial screening; it does not identify which works are small or establish suitability for nature‑based treatment. If we publish an edition that adds the figures, buyers of this one get it free.
  • Exact location. Positions are accurate to about a kilometre, which is enough to plan a visit and not enough to find the gate.
  • Scheme delivery. We have not confirmed who will deliver each scheme. In the public sources we checked on 30 October 2026, we found December 2025 appointments naming Kier for early contractor involvement at seven of the 95 works, and no named contractor for the other 88. Southern Water’s AMP8 non‑infrastructure delivery partners are CMDP+ (Costain and MWH Treatment), Kier Integrated Services and Galliford Try: the likely routes to the work, not confirmed owners of any particular scheme. The next edition adds a named‑contractor column.
  • A ranking that knows your business. Ours is a simple, transparent starting point. The dictionary explains every weight so you can adjust the ranking.
