Privacy

Who we are

SectorFlare is a trading name of EdgeScale Advisory Ltd (company number 17086813), which is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at awais@sectorflare.co.uk.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. Its fonts are served from the site itself, not a third party. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers record visitors’ IP addresses for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

If we’ve emailed you

We contact people at UK companies whose work relates to our data packs, using business contact details published on company websites or other public sources. We only email business addresses at incorporated companies.

  • What we hold: your name, role, company, business email or phone number, and our correspondence with you.
  • Why: our legitimate interest in telling businesses about products relevant to their work.
  • How long: up to two years after our last contact with you, whether or not you replied. That covers sample requests, enquiries and order correspondence; billing records have their own period below. After that we delete it, keeping only the address of anyone who asked us not to write again.
  • Opting out: reply “no” or “unsubscribe” to any email, or tell us any other way. We’ll stop marketing emails straight away and keep your address on a suppression list so that we don’t contact you again. If you have bought a pack, we also keep the order and billing records described below.

If you ask for a sample or enquire about a pack

We keep your request and our reply with the correspondence described above, and use them only to answer you.

If you buy a pack

We keep your billing contact and invoice details for six years from the invoice date, as UK tax law requires, and use them only to deliver the pack, invoice you and answer questions about your order.

Who else sees your data

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t pass it to anyone else. Two services process it on our behalf: Microsoft 365, supplied through GoDaddy, which carries our email; and GitHub Pages, which hosts this site and logs visitors’ IP addresses. Both are US companies and may process data outside the UK; their transfer safeguards are described in Microsoft’s privacy statement and GitHub’s.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. Email us and we’ll reply within a month. If you’re not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
